I am a highly effective coach, mentor and creative partner for artists, strategists (managers, promoters, producers) and venues. My multi-disciplinary background enables me to provide creative and technical solutions for complex projects with many moving parts. 

Providing light, clarity and helping decisions to be made. I have worn many hats over the last twenty years. Artist, venue founder, record label owner, artist manager, programmer, promoter, tour manager, creative producer, record producer (to name a few). Building a career in the music industry is challenging and there is no one single path. 

The way forward I found was entirely independent.Through my work I was meeting many highly credentialed people who, after leaving years of music education, had no concept of how to navigate the commercial world of the music industry. So, since 2020 I have been on a mission to try to figure out what was not working in music business education and to make change.This has led me to senior lecture roles at BIMM, ICMP, University of Westminster and the creation of a new role created for me at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama as the Professor of Music Business.
